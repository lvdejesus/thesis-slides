Objectives

  1. To determine to which tokens, layers, and attention or MLP sublayers the update must be applied for the concept to appear for all fine-tuned models.
  2. To determine which attributes depend on the trigger, and trace how information about the trigger reaches the image tokens in terms of tokens, attention paths, sublayers, and denoising steps.
  3. To test these findings by editing weight updates, constructing weight updates, constructing triggers for existing fine-tuned models, and comparing the outcome with a predicted result.
